Security & Posture

Trust & Security Foundation

Your medical documents contain your most personal information. Here is how Healthie protects your privacy at every step — thoroughly explained in plain terms.

🔒

Zero PHI Leakage

Your personal health information (PHI) is isolated in your private ContextEngine storage namespace. It is never used to train public AI models or shared across users.

🛡️
HIPAA-Aligned Posture

Built with AWS KMS AES-256 encryption at rest, TLS 1.3 encryption in transit, strict IAM access controls, and CloudTrail audit logging.

🔬

PubMed Tier-1 Citations

Healthie does not guess or make up answers. Every clinical finding is grounded directly in peer-reviewed PubMed and NIH literature with full citation links.

🩺

Fact vs Interpretation Safety

Our Safety Agent strictly separates observed facts from potential interpretations. Healthie never diagnoses or prescribes — it prepares you to consult your doctor.
